A Different Story About AI

The question is not how much intelligence we can extract. It is what becomes more alive because of it.

The dominant AI conversation treats intelligence like another resource to mine: automate the person, compress the relationship, capture the knowledge, increase the yield.

Regenerative AI starts somewhere else. It asks whether technology can return more capacity, agency, continuity, creativity, resilience, and shared intelligence to the system around it.

I use ancestral intelligence as a grounding counterweight: the accumulated human wisdom of relationship, reciprocity, place, consequence, stewardship, and knowing when not to optimize.

Why Rick

I have spent more than 30 years watching systems work, scale, fracture, and get rebuilt.

Technology. Operations. Construction. Real estate. Entrepreneurship. Human transformation. Different environments, same pattern: when the architecture underneath something stops matching what it has become, people start compensating with their bodies, attention, memory, and relationships.

My work sits where regenerative purpose, practical execution, technology, human behavior, and lived experience meet. I am not interested in making people more efficient cogs in a machine. Direct Answers

What this work means in practice.

What is Regenerative AI?

AI designed to increase the health and capacity of the system around it: more human agency, better memory, clearer judgment, more creative possibility, stronger relationships, and less unnecessary extraction.

What does “ancestral intelligence” mean here?

The accumulated human intelligence of reciprocity, stewardship, consequence, relationship, place, embodiment, story, and knowing that not everything valuable should be optimized for maximum yield.

How do founders work with Rick?

Through the Four-Step Regenerative Systems Pathway, the monthly Founder Lab, or deeper QuickLaunch implementation work depending on the stage and pressure.

Where does Saberra fit?

Saberra is where the regenerative AI thesis becomes operational: a human-accountable AI workforce and organizational intelligence layer built around memory, continuity, roles, and distributed responsibility.
